Where races live: every memo write goes through useUpdateMemo in web/src/hooks/useMemoQueries.ts, whose onSuccess writes the server's reply into memoKeys.detail (staleTime 10 s) and into every list page without checking for a newer in-flight update. The unguarded callers are in web/src/components/MemoActionMenu/hooks.ts (pin, archive/restore, check-all/uncheck-all tasks) and web/src/components/MemoReactionListView/hooks.ts (reactions: raw client calls, then a getMemo refetch written over the detail). The memo editor's save is guarded (EditSession.tsx, isLoading.saving; commit button disabled), and tag rename does not exist in the current sidebar (#6455), so skip both. Important for explore: useLiveMemoRefresh.ts streams /api/sse through fetch and on memo.changed invalidates memoKeys.all with refetchType active, so a stale cache is corrected soon after every write; a convincing timeline shows the stale value outliving that refetch, or holds the refetch too and says so. Query defaults (web/src/lib/query-client.ts): staleTime 30 s, refetchOnWindowFocus true, one retry.

Tests: Vitest 4 with jsdom, files under web/tests/*.test.{ts,tsx}, setup in web/tests/setup.ts, mockReset and restoreMocks on. Follow web/tests/use-memo-save.test.tsx and query-deduplication.test.tsx: vi.hoisted mocks of @/connect service clients, a QueryClientProvider wrapper, renderHook and act. web/tests/memo-record.test.tsx already covers optimistic patches, so extend near it.
